# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in Agent Quarter to be welcoming and respectful for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or role.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive and specific in issues, reviews, and discussions.
- Respect different experience levels and good-faith disagreement.
- Keep feedback about ideas and code, never personal attacks.
- Protect others' privacy and never share credentials, private data, or internal work without permission.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or abusive language.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberately sharing malware, secrets, or exploit instructions outside responsible disclosure.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to repository maintainers through GitHub. Maintainers may remove content, reject contributions, or limit participation when needed to protect project community.
